The library required for encoding AAC-LC and HE-AAC V1/V2 streams is not present on the host machine.

This engine offers professional audio quality, infinitely richer and more precise than standard encoders. For legal reasons, it is not possible to include it directly in this project.

You have two options to obtain this library:

  • Automatic download: Click the button below to download the DLL.
